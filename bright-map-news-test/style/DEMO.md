# Bright Map News: demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

**Story (21.5 s, 1080x1920, no voice):** "Three days of heavy rain: where does the water go?" in five beats on one SE-Asia map: (1) hook: pin on Bangkok, card rolls to 312 mm, (2) orange dashed route draws from Chiang Mai to the Gulf with city pins, (3) push-in on the central plain, neighbours dim, a card says 10-14 days, (4) pull-back, neighbouring countries fill in five blues with a legend and a blue card, (5) silence in the music and a text-only verdict caption. All figures are illustrative samples.

**Look:** pale-blue sea, white/ice land, orange subject, navy bug and type, paper caption panel (see ../STYLE.md).
**Score:** 110 BPM synthesized bed (soft kick, hats, bass, marimba-like plucks, warm pad); music cuts out 16.9-17.9 s; foley on every graphic action (chunk click, pin pop, whoosh, counter ticks, pings on route cities and fills).
**Native moves shown:** pin and card (0.4 s, 9.9 s), chunk slam with orange keyword (each caption), route draw (5.2-8.4 s), region fill (14.2-16 s), push-in (9-10 s).
**Files:** `demo/index.html` draws everything (`CAPS` captions, `CAM` camera keys); `demo/audio/mix.py` writes the score; `demo/build.sh` renders and muxes (`OUT=... sh build.sh`).
**Known nits:** the Chiang Mai label sits under the bug at 11 s; the Cambodia label is low-contrast on dark blue.
